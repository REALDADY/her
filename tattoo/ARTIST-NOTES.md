| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| N + compass rose | direction / purpose |
| Atlas mountains, sun, birds | where it started |
| Moroccan town with minaret, palms | home, roots |

| Dubai skyline (Burj Khalifa, Burj Al Arab) | where I am now |
| Laptop `</>` | the work – coding |
| Olive branch | peace, growth |

| Hourglass | time |
| Moroccan star | where I come from |

## Changes from the AI draft
- Removed muddy cross-hatching; shading is now dotwork (stipple), which heals cleanly and ages well.
- Lines are never closer than about 1 mm at ~9 cm wide, so they won't blur together.
- Clear light direction (from the left): every shadow falls on the right-hand faces.
- Tidied the anatomy, buildings, wave and surfer so they read at small size.
- Lettering changed to a handwritten script so it looks personal rather than generated.

## Size
Designed as a vertical piece around **8–10 cm wide × 23–28 cm tall** (spine, forearm or ribs).
Under ~7 cm wide, simplify the town windows and the skyline reflections.
Use `lifeline-tattoo.svg` for the stencil – it scales without losing quality.
